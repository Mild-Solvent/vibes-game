# Market landscape: friendslop (scan dated 2026-09-29)

## What "friendslop" is
Cheap (<$20) online co-op, low skill floor, **proximity voice chat**, physics jank,
low-fi 3D, one-sentence premise, no fun solo. The fun comes from players talking to
each other. The term started in 2025 as a joke and devs now use it proudly.

## Numbers that matter
- 2025: 4 of the 10 best-selling Steam games by copies were friendslop:
  R.E.P.O. 18.5M, Peak 15.4M, Schedule I 9.1M, RV There Yet? 5.7M.
- 2026: Gamble With Your Friends sold 1M in its first week. Big Walk (House House) sold
  2M by 25 Sep. Meccha Chameleon is the biggest friendslop hit of the year so far.
- **D30 retention is about 3%** (Content Warning 3%, Phasmophobia 5.3%, Dead by
  Daylight 11.3%). People play hard for 2 weeks and leave. Consequences for us:
  - skip deep meta-progression; put effort into the core loop
  - **content updates bring streamers back** (Super Battle Golf creators returned
    5 to 6 times after launch), so plan the launch plus a steady update rhythm
- Price band: $8 to $15. Super Battle Golf $8, Roadside Research $13.
- Streamer-first hooks win. Roadside Research's trailer was built like an interview
  from *The Office*, made so creators would pick it up.

## Nearest neighbours to our concept, and how we differ
- **Content Warning**: you film horror for views. It's found footage, recorded then
  uploaded. **We are live**: no retakes, a crew with separate roles, and the audience
  reacts in real time.
- **Acting Out**: players are *actors* filming scripted scenes, then watch the movie.
  **We are the crew**. In our game the actors (NPCs or one player) are the least
  important people, and the laughs come from behind the scenes.
- **Foul Play / BattleBlock Theater**: they use a theatre as a setting. Nobody plays the crew.

## Gaps we searched and found empty
