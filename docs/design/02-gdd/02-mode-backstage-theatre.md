# Mode: Places, Please! (backstage theatre crew)

> Opening night at the Grand Municipal Theatre. The lead actor is drunk, the fog
> machine is possessed, and the audience can hear everything.

## Setting
A mid-size theatre cut in half by the curtain line:
- **Stage** under the lights, which the audience sees.
- **Wings** left and right, dark with glow-tape marks, with prop tables.
- **Fly loft**: ropes and counterweights that fly backdrops and set pieces in and out.
- **Trap room** under the stage: trapdoors, lift, and the ghost entrance.
- **Booth** at the back of the house: lighting board and sound desk, with a view of the stage.
- **Dressing rooms** / corridor (the quick-change station).

## Roles
| Role | Where | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage manager** | Wings, holding the prompt book | Calls cues on headset ("Standby LX 12... GO"), prompts actors who dry |
| **Fly crew** | Fly loft | Pull the right numbered rope at the right moment. Wrong rope = the forest drops into the castle |
| **Deck crew ×1–2** | Wings / stage in blackouts | Shift set pieces **in the dark** during scene changes, hand props to actors |
| **Booth op** | Booth | Lights and sound effects (thunder, doorbell, the "wrong" sound effect) |
| **Dresser** | Quick-change corner | Get the actor from King to Beggar in 20 s |
| **Actor** (optional player talent) | Stage | Performs lines fed by the stage manager / prompt book |

NPC actors fill all remaining roles. They're dumb and follow marks exactly. If you
move their mark (glow tape) they walk to the wrong place.

## Core loop per scene
1. **Scene plays**: NPC or player actors perform under lights. Crew prepare the next
   change quietly, whispering in the wings.
2. **Cue calls**: the stage manager calls lights, sound and fly cues on the timeline.
   Each one is hit, late, wrong or missed.
3. **Blackout scene change**: 10–20 s of near darkness. The deck crew must move set
   pieces to the glow-tape marks, and the whole audience is listening. This is the core
   panic moment.
4. **Lights up**: the audience judges what's there. A half-moved sofa, a crew member
   frozen in the light, a chair on the wrong mark.

## Signature gags
- **Caught in the light.** Lights come up while you're still on stage. The game gives
  you a choice: freeze and pretend to be a tree (Meccha Chameleon energy), or run.
  Freezing well *can* score as a "happy accident".
- **The dry.** The actor forgets their line, and the stage manager has to whisper it
  from the wings. Too quiet and the actor doesn't hear. Too loud and the audience
  does.
- **Wrong rope.** 40 ropes, all numbered, some labels fell off. Backdrop roulette.
- **Quick change.** A costume minigame on physics bodies: jam an NPC into trousers
  while they're walking back on stage.
- **Wrong sound cue.** A doorbell during the death scene. The sound board is a grid
  of labelled buttons, and someone swapped the labels.
- **Audience noise.** Loud talking backstage raises a "shhh" meter in the front rows.
  Full meter means the audience hears it, and it's on the Tape.

## Plays (content = new plays, same theatre)
Each play = a script of scenes + a set kit (backdrops, set pieces, props, costumes).
- **Hamlet (abridged, badly)**: castle, graveyard, skull prop, sword fight, ghost via trapdoor
- **The Nativity (school play)**: tiny sets, a live donkey (physics nightmare), a star on a wire
- **Space Musical**: a flying spaceship, a smoke machine, a laser cue storm
- **Murder Mystery**: the body must stay still (it's a player)
- **Panto**: "He's behind you!" The audience literally points at crew

## Tape
The audience view from the best seat, plus a "balcony cam" of the wings for bloopers.
