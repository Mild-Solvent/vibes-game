# Roadmap & release strategy (draft)

## Release strategy: recommendation
**One game, two launch modes, then content as updates.** It's not "one mode now, the
other as DLC".
- Launch with **both** We're Live! and Places, Please!, because "two shows" makes the
  pitch ("you're the crew of a live show") read as a *system*, not a gimmick. If scope
  bites, launch with the funnier one after playtests and ship the other as **Update 1**.
- **Free content updates** every 6–8 weeks (new show = new mode). With about 3% D30
  retention, updates are what bring streamers back, and streamers are the marketing.
- **Paid** stuff: a cosmetic supporter pack at launch; paid DLC only for a big new
  show later (e.g. Kids' TV puppets) once there's an audience. At a $10–13 base
  price, early paid DLC feels bad and fragments lobbies (friendslop needs everyone in
  the same lobby).

## Phases
### 0. Pre-production (now)
- [x] Market scan, concept, GDD drafts
- [ ] Artist reads the docs and pushes back; agree on art style (a moodboard)
- [ ] Confirm engine (Unity 6 proposed)
- [ ] Pick the working title; check Steam, domain and trademark

### 1. Tech spikes (1–2 weeks)
See [engine-decision](../01-concept/engine-decision.md#tech-spikes-before-committing-see-roadmap).
T1 Steam lobby + synced physics · T2 proximity voice + live mic bus · T3 camera
switcher to broadcast RT · T4 Tape record/replay. **T2 and T4 are the risks.** If they
fail, the concept changes.

### 2. Grey-box prototype (3–4 weeks)
- One grey-box studio + one grey-box theatre, no art
- One show script each, 3–4 players
- **Playtest with real friends on voice every week.** The only question is: *did we laugh?*
- Record every playtest; the funniest moments decide what gets built next

### 3. Vertical slice (4–6 weeks)
- The first real art from the artist on one mode
- WRAP screen, The Tape playback, lobby
- Cut a **trailer draft from real Tape clips**

### 4. Steam page + demo
- Steam page up as early as there's one good GIF (wishlists compound)
- A demo for **Steam Next Fest** (Crashout Crew's Next Fest demo is what made it take off)
- Send keys to small/mid streamers who play friendslop (list to build)

### 5. Launch → updates
- Early Access vs 1.0: decide at the vertical slice. EA fits the "update" rhythm.
