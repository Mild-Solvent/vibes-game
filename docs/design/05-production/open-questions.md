# Open questions

Decide these as we go. Record each answer + date in `decisions.md` when made.

## Big ones
1. **Engine:** Unity 6 (proposed) vs Godot. See engine-decision.md.
2. **Launch scope:** both modes at launch, or one + an update? Decide after the prototype playtests.
3. **Working title.**
4. **Is the Talent a player or NPC by default?** A player talent = funnier improv, but one
   person is "stuck" at the desk. Maybe the default is NPC talent and "Talent" is a
   selectable role.
5. **Prompter typing:** real keyboard typing (funny typos, but that player can't use
   voice much) vs. picking lines from options.
6. **How is "said on air" scored?** Honour system + Tape moments (v1) vs speech-to-text
   (later, maybe never).

## Smaller
- Player count cap: 6 or 8?
- Do NPC talent/actors have voice lines? (TTS gibberish à la Animal Crossing is cheap and funny.)
- Crossplay / console: ignore for now.
- Localisation: English first. The prompter mode is very text-heavy, so check keyboard
  layouts (Slovak/Czech diacritics!).
- Moderation: players can type anything on the prompter, and it goes on the Tape and
  the stream. Filter + vote-kick + streamer-safe mode.
- The artist's credit / revenue split: agree it in writing early, even between
  partners. It's cheap to do now and awkward later.
