# More shows (future modes / updates / DLC)

The umbrella is **"you are the crew of a live show."** Every new show reuses the core
(roles, live mics, cue sheet, audience meter, The Tape) and only needs a new set kit,
a run-of-show and a few unique gags. That's why this concept scales so well with
content updates.

Rated on: **Hook** (streamer appeal) · **Reuse** (how much shared tech covers it) ·
**Art** (asset load for the artist). ★ = strongest candidates.

## Suggested order
1. **Launch:** We're Live! + Places, Please! (1 set each, 3 shows or plays each)
2. **Update 1 (+6–8 weeks):** Teleshopping (reuses the TV set, cheap, very clippable)
3. **Update 2:** Magic Show (reuses the theatre)
4. **Seasonal:** Halloween Paranormal Special / Christmas Special (timed with the calendar)
5. **Bigger update or paid DLC:** Kids' TV puppets (new tech: puppet physics), Game Show + PvP Rival Networks

## Loose gags / mechanics to keep
- Applause/"LAUGH" signs held by crew. If the audience laughs at the wrong moment, the meter goes haywire.
- The station owner / theatre director NPC turns up in PREP and makes absurd demands
  (the show's "modifier" for the round).
- **Modifiers** (roguelite-lite variety): "the lead has a cold", "sponsor product must
  appear 5 times", "blackout: no work lights", "the guest is a real horse".
- Crew cosmetics: headsets, hi-vis, all-black "stage blacks" (which ironically help
  you hide in the dark).
- A lobby TV that shows your crew's best Tape clips (the hall of shame).
- Bleep sound customisation: duck, horn, a classical-music sting.
