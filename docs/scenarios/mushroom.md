# Scenario: Mushroom Foraging (one round)

How a round should feel in the finished game. The last section says what exists today.

## Setting
A spruce forest somewhere in Slovakia, late September, four friends and one green Škoda.
The basket sits by the car. The forest spreads out in every direction, the fog is already
coming in, and in four minutes it will be dark. Every good mushroom has an evil twin.

## Player roles (2-8)
Nobody is assigned a job, but jobs happen anyway:
- **The Expert**: read the field guide once in 2009. Shouts names with great confidence.
- **The Runner**: sprints mushrooms back to the basket, throws them the last ten metres.
- **The Taster**: presses F. Usually by choice.
- **The Lost One**: wanders off for "one more porcini" and is never seen in daylight again.
- **The Driver** (later): stays near the car, keeps the headlights on as a lighthouse.

## One round, minute by minute
- **-0:20 PREP.** Everyone stands by the car reading the field guide in the corner of the
  screen. Porcini: brown cap, pale stem. Satan's bolete: greyer cap, RED stem. Parasol
  (bedľa): big flat cap, thin tall stem. Death cap (muchotrávka zelená): greenish, smaller.
  Chanterelle (kuriatko): yellow-gold. False chanterelle: deeper orange. Nobody reads it.
- **0:00 FORAGING.** Daylight, light fog. Four people sprint in four directions.
- **0:25** Janka finds a fat brown porcini, carries it with both hands like a newborn and
  places it in the basket. +10. It is the best moment of her life.
- **0:50** Miro finds "a parasol". It is small, greenish and has a ring. "Bedľa, 100%."
  He drops it in the basket. -20. The BASKET bar visibly flinches.
- **1:20** Denis finds a bright orange chanterelle. "Is this one the fake?" Nobody knows.
  Denis presses F. "That was a False chanterelle..." The world begins to wobble and
  slowly change colour. "Somebody tasted the wrong one..." shows for everyone.
- **1:30** Tripping Denis reports that the trees are breathing and that the basket is
  talking to him. He walks confidently into a spruce. Twice.
- **2:00** Dusk. The fog thickens, the sky goes grey-blue. Janka throws a porcini to Miro
  from fifteen metres. It hits him in the face. He drops a Satan's bolete he was not
  supposed to be holding anyway.
- **2:40** Miro, to save face, tastes a "porcini" with a red stem to prove it is fine.
  It is not fine. Two people are now tripping. One of them is guiding the other home.
- **3:15** Nearly dark. The car's warm glow is the only landmark. Everyone hears Denis
  shouting "I found the car!" from the opposite direction of the car.
- **3:50** Janka sprints in with three chanterelles, overshoots, throws them into the
  basket from the side. Two land in. One lands on the Škoda's roof.
- **4:00 BACK AT THE CAR.** "5 good, 1 poisonous." The poisonous one is the death cap. Denis is still in the forest.

## Win and lose conditions
- **Score** = points in the basket when it gets dark. Porcini +10, parasol +8,
  chanterelle +5. Satan's bolete -15, death cap -20, false chanterelle -8.
- **Win**: a good basket, zero poisonous ones (the "Grandma Approves" ending).
- **Lose**: basket at zero, or a death cap in the basket (finished game: grandma cooks
  it, the WRAP screen is a hospital waiting room).
- Tasting a good one is safe but it is eaten, so those points are gone.
- Finished game: anyone not back at the car at dark counts as "lost" and costs points.

## The Tape (end-of-round replay)
A shaky "family video" camcorder cut with a date stamp in the corner:
- Janka cradling her first porcini, slow motion, choir music.
- Miro's "Bedľa, 100%" followed by the death cap close-up and a sad trombone.
- Denis's trip from his own point of view: wobbling trees, swapped colours, the spruce.
- The overhead fog map showing everyone's paths: three neat loops and one Denis spiral.
- Final shot: the basket contents laid out on the car bonnet, poison ones circled in red.

## Demo today vs still missing

**Demo today (games/mushroom)**
- Fixed-seed forest: 160 spruces, 48 mushrooms, six kinds (three good, three look-alikes).
- Pick and carry (E), throw (Q), taste (F); basket by the car scores what is inside it.
- Bad taste: 25 s wobbly, colour-shifting screen for that player, banner for everyone.
- Good taste: "Tasty, and you're fine" toast; the mushroom is gone either way.
- 4-minute round: sun dims, fog thickens and goes dark; WRAP shows good / poisonous count.
- Field guide in the HUD, up to 8 players over LAN.

**Still missing**
- Real hallucinations (fake mushrooms, talking basket, moving trees), not just a filter.
- Torches or headlights as a real tool, getting lost, "not back at the car" penalty.
- Closer look-alikes (rings, smell, cutting a stem) and more Slovak species.
- Voice chat so lost friends can shout; any Tape or replay at all.
- Endings (grandma, hospital), and a basket score that is not capped at 100.
